      About the evidence

      Conclusion

      Together, the 24 stories show that reducing vulnerabilities does more than relieve immediate hardship. It creates the conditions for sustained change by strengthening people's capabilities, widening opportunities and improving access to resources and services, so that individuals and households can build resilience and hope for the future.

      Read with care

      • The consolidation was AI-generated from the submitted stories and should be checked against them.
      • The evidence is qualitative, based on individual experiences. The stories illustrate plausible pathways; sustained impact needs follow-up evidence.
      • Long-term case studies (indicator 01) were not reported in 2026. They would test whether these changes last.
      • Disability inclusion appears strongly in the stories but has no dedicated framework indicator yet.

      Source: Consolidated Assessment of 24 Stories, 2026 (AI-generated synthesis), with the stories of Tamela Kidawenda, Wambura, and a Tanzania (CD24 TNZ) community project among those cited. Indicator results come from the results dashboard.
